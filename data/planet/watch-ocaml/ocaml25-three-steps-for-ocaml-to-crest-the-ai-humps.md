<p>Three steps for OCaml to crest the AI humps (Video, OCaml 2025)<br/>
Sadiq Jaffer, Jonathan Ludlam, Ryan Gibb, Thomas Gazagnaire, and Anil Madhavapeddy<br/>
(University of Cambridge; University of Cambridge; University of Cambridge; Tarides; University of Cambridge, UK)</p>
<p>Abstract: We discuss how OCaml could adapt to the fast-moving world of AI-assisted agentic coding. We first benchmark how well represented OCaml is in the large and diverse set of open weight models that can be run locally. We then consider what is unique about OCaml programming (in particular, modules and abstraction) that differentiates it in this space. We then consider the changes required in our ecosystem to work better with AI coding assistants.</p>
