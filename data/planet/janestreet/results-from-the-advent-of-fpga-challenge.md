<p>At the end of last year, we decided to try something new: a
<a href="https://blog.janestreet.com/advent-of-fpga-challenge-2025/">challenge</a> that would run
alongside <a href="https://adventofcode.com/">Advent of Code</a>, where we asked the community to
show us how they could design hardware to solve the same problems. We had no idea what
level of participation to expect, but we received a huge number of submissions, many of
which were incredibly creative!</p>
