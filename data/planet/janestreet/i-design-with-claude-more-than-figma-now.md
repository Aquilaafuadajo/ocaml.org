<p>For a long time I was skeptical of LLMs&mdash;whenever I reached for them I was disappointed by
the results. Last year I tried Copilot and Cursor to tweak a game I&rsquo;d built, and neither
generated working changes. At a previous job I tried Gemini to outline product briefs and
generate wireframes, but ended up throwing them all away. Every time I tried LLMs it was
for something I was already good at, and they did a worse job than I would have.</p>
